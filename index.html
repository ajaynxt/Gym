Frequently asked questions

Remove doubts before the sales call

Good FAQs reduce repetitive enquiries and help qualified leads take action faster.

Is the free trial really free?

This demo flow assumes one complimentary trial session. A real gym must state duration, eligibility and booking conditions.

Can beginners join?

Yes. Visitors can select their goal and current level during the trial enquiry so the team can recommend a suitable plan.

Are personal training and diet guidance included?

They depend on the selected plan. Each plan should clearly list inclusions and additional charges.

Can supplements be purchased online?

This demo creates a WhatsApp order request. A real client can connect payment, inventory, invoices and shipping later.

Do you provide women-only timings?

The schedule can include women-focused sessions or timings based on the actual gym’s operations.
